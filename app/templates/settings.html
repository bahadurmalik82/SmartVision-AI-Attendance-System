Frequently asked questions

How do I add a class?

On the Students page, use "Create a New Class" — one class is one section of one semester of one department (for example BS Computer Science · Section B · Semester 5). When the section moves to the next semester, create a new class; the old one keeps its history.

How do courses work inside a class?

Courses live in the class's Weekly Timetable, not as separate classes. Add each course to the timetable (day, time, course name) and attendance is recorded per course. One class can hold many courses — for example Machine Learning, Operating Systems and Web Engineering in the same section.

How does the camera switch on the Dashboard work?

Pick a camera from the dropdown and only the video area changes — the page does not reload. "Primary Active" is the camera a live session uses; you can switch the live view to any other camera for that session without changing the primary. Browser/USB cameras use your laptop's camera; Wireless/IP Stream cameras use a phone or IP camera feed.

What is the 75% rule?

University policy needs at least 75% attendance to sit for the final exam. Students below 75% appear on the Defaulters page; below 70% they are "Barred from Final Exam", between 70% and 75% they are on "Borderline Warning". You can change the 75% threshold in the Attendance policy card above.

How do transcript emails work?

On the Defaulters page, each student has a "Send Transcript" button, and you can tick several students and use "Send Email" to send transcripts to all of them at once. A transcript lists the student's per-course attendance. Students without an email on file are skipped and named in the result message.

What do I do if email is not configured?

Set SMTP in the .env file (see the Email alerts card above for the free Gmail steps), restart the app, then press "Send me a test email" here. Until then, the Send Alert Email buttons will tell you email is not set up — nothing is sent silently.

How does face enrolment work?

On the Students page, open a class and capture each student's face from three angles (front, left, right) using the camera, or upload photos in bulk. Attenova stores a 128-D face embedding for each student — not the photo itself for matching — and recognises them during a live class. After enrolling, re-train the ML models from the ML Models page.

About Attenova

AI-powered smart university attendance & duration tracking — v9.3

Attenova replaces the paper register with face recognition. A teacher starts a class, the camera recognises each student as they sit down, and attendance — including how long each student was actually present — is recorded automatically, per course, per class.

Multi-camera switching

Wireless/IP Stream cameras (a phone as an IP webcam) and Browser/USB cameras, side by side. Switch the live view from the Dashboard without reloading the page; the primary camera stays as set.

Face recognition

Each student is enrolled from three angles (front, left, right) and stored as a 128-D HOG face embedding. Four classifiers — SVM, KNN, Random Forest, Logistic Regression — are benchmarked on the ML Models page.

Timetable auto-attendance

The Weekly Timetable starts sessions automatically on schedule, including Sundays, and can pause a class for a holiday. Presence is tracked in 10-minute blocks, so duration — not just a tick — is recorded.

Per-course analytics

Analytics breaks attendance down by course, section, date and student, with K-Means clustering (k = 3) that groups students by attendance and lateness, and an Excel export of any report.

Defaulters, transcripts & drop alerts

Students below the 75% threshold are listed with an ML risk label; the teacher can email a warning or a full attendance transcript to one student or many. If a student's attendance drops into a worse band, the warning goes out automatically.

Student portal

Every student gets a CMS ID and a password at registration and can log in to the Student Portal to see their own attendance, per course, without seeing anyone else's.
