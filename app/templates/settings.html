About Attenova

Attenova v7.0 — AI-powered smart attendance and duration tracking. Built with Flask + SQLite. 4 ML models benchmarked (SVM, KNN, Random Forest, Logistic Regression) on 128-D face embeddings. Presence is tracked in 10-minute blocks, and 75% attendance is needed for final-exam eligibility.

Data & privacy

Student portal passwords are only visible to the student's own teacher. A backup contains the attendance database only — student photos live in the data/student_photos/ folder and are not included.
