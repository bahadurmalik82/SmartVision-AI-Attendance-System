Multi-Camera Configuration & Streaming

Connect and switch between your Laptop Webcam, external USB cameras, or Mobile Phone running IP Webcam over Wi-Fi.

How to use your Mobile Phone as a Wireless Classroom Camera ($0 Cost):
  1. Make sure your mobile phone and this PC are connected to the same Wi-Fi network (or phone hotspot).
  2. Open the "IP Webcam" app on your Android/iPhone and scroll down to click "Start server".
  3. The app will display an IP address on your phone screen (e.g. http://192.168.1.15:8080).
  4. Add it below using URL format: http://<phone_ip>:8080/video and click Test Connection.
