Everything a classroom needs

Built for a real lecture hall — a teacher's laptop camera or a phone on the wall is enough.

Face Recognition

128-D HOG face embeddings identify each enrolled student from 3-angle photos, with a blur check and anti-spoof liveness detection.

Live Duration Tracking

Presence is tracked in 10-minute blocks from the moment class starts until it ends — not just a single snapshot at the door.

ML Model Benchmark

SVM, KNN, Random Forest and Logistic Regression are benchmarked with 5-fold cross-validation, confusion matrices, precision, recall and F1-score.

Defaulter Prediction

K-Means clusters students into Star, Inconsistent and At-Risk, and a Logistic Regression model flags students heading below the 75% eligibility line.

Analytics & Reports

Filter attendance by course, section, date or student, correct a record manually, and export Excel reports for the exam office.

Student Self-Service

Every student gets a CMS ID and password, logs in, and sees their own attendance per subject — absences with dates, and what to attend to recover.

How it works

Three steps, and the class runs itself.

1

Teacher sets up once

Register, add your weekly timetable, connect a camera, and enrol students — one by one or by CSV. Each student gets a CMS ID and password automatically.

2

Class runs itself

The scheduler starts monitoring exactly when class starts. The camera recognises faces, checks liveness, and logs presence in 10-minute blocks until class ends.

3

Everyone sees the truth

The teacher sees analytics and defaulters; the student logs in with their CMS ID and sees their own attendance, absences and what to attend to stay eligible.
